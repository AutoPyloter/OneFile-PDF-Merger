Privacy-First Offline PDF Tool

Why OneFile?

OneFile is a dedicated offline PDF merger built for professionals who prioritize data privacy. Unlike traditional cloud-based services, this no upload PDF tool processes your documents entirely within your web browser. Your sensitive files never leave your device, ensuring maximum security and confidentiality.

100% Client-Side Processing

By utilizing modern WebAssembly (via pdf-lib) and the power of your device's RAM, this client-side PDF application allows you to merge PDFs and compress images instantly. There's no server involved, which means zero waiting for uploads or downloads—even for large, high-resolution documents.

Completely Offline Capable

Save this page or download our self-contained HTML file to merge your files completely offline. Our privacy-first PDF merge utility requires no internet connection after the initial load, no registration, and absolutely no subscriptions. Fast, free, and secure.
